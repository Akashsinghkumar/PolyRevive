Recycled PET Flakes

High-quality recycled PET flakes produced from post-consumer PET waste after sorting, washing, separation, and processing.

Our flakes are processed through rigorous cleaning and drying steps to minimize moisture, eliminate labels and adhesives, and maintain the chemical integrity required for secondary processing.

Potential Applications:

Fibre manufacturing Strapping Sheet production Packaging-related applications Industrial applications Further PET processing

Recycled PET Pellets

Processed recycled PET material designed for industries requiring consistent recycled polymer feedstock.

Engineered through melt-processing and extrusion of clean PET flakes, delivering uniform granules suitable for standard injection and extrusion machinery without altering factory workflows.
