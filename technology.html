PROCESSING ECOSYSTEM

Our Planned Processing Ecosystem

From Section 6 of Official Company Documentation: Key operational pillars ensuring consistent recycled PET output.

Efficient PET Processing

High-performance mechanical systems designed for continuous, high-volume processing of post-consumer PET bottles.

Advanced Washing Systems

Specialized multi-stage washing to thoroughly eliminate labels, adhesives, dirt, and foreign organic matter.

Material Separation

High-efficiency density and float-sink separation ensuring non-PET polymers (like polyolefin caps) are cleanly segregated.

Moisture Management

Engineered drying systems achieving exact target moisture levels required for downstream industrial use.

Contamination Reduction

Systematic inline screening and multi-stage filtration to minimize particulate and chemical contaminants.

Consistent Output Quality

Uniform flake sizing and pellet intrinsic viscosity tailored for rigorous manufacturing specifications.

Responsible Water Management

Closed-loop water recycling and wastewater treatment reducing fresh water consumption across washing operations.

Process Monitoring & QC

Continuous process monitoring and laboratory inspection covering contamination, flake purity, and customer parameters.

OUR INFRASTRUCTURE

Designed for Efficient Recycling

PolyRevive's proposed recycling ecosystem is designed to integrate the major components required for comprehensive PET processing.

PET sorting and processing area Dedicated sorting bays for incoming PET waste.
PET crushing system Heavy-duty granulators reducing bottles into flakes.
Washing and separation systems Friction and thermal washing with density segregation.
Drying system Centrifugal and thermal drying for moisture removal.
Flake processing line Converting cleaned PET into high-grade flakes.
Pelletizing system Extrusion and degassing for recycled polymer pellets.
Material storage Protected storage for raw baled PET bottles and scrap.
Finished-product storage Clean, contamination-free silos and warehouse space.
Quality-control facilities In-house inspection for testing flake purity and metrics.
Water treatment and recycling Multi-stage filtration and recirculating water loop.
Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) Ensuring strict environmental and discharge compliance.
Logistics & material-handling Forklifts, conveyors, and docks for seamless transport.
